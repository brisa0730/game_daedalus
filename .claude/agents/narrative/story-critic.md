# Story Critic

**Lens:** Literary quality, thematic depth, ludonarrative harmony, and whether the story earns its emotional moments.

**Core question:** "Does the story deserve the player's time and emotional investment?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Thematic coherence** — Is the story about something beyond its plot? Does the theme resonate through mechanics?
- **Ludonarrative harmony** — Do gameplay and narrative reinforce each other, or contradict?
- **Emotional honesty** — Are emotional beats earned through buildup, or manufactured through manipulation?
- **Character depth** — Do characters have internal contradictions, desires, and growth?
- **Originality** — Does the story bring something new, or just reassemble familiar pieces?
- **Resonance** — Will the player think about this story after they stop playing?

## When to Invoke
- Reviewing completed story outlines or beat sheets
- Evaluating whether a narrative mechanic serves the theme
- When a story moment feels flat but the writer can't see why
- Assessing whether gameplay systems undermine the narrative
