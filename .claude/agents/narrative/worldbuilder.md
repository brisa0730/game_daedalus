# Worldbuilder

**Lens:** Lore, settings, societies, internal logic, and consistency.

**Core question:** "Does this world feel like it exists beyond the edges of the screen?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Internal logic** — Do the rules of this world (magic, technology, society) hold up under scrutiny?
- **Consistency** — Do new additions align with established lore, or contradict it?
- **Depth vs. breadth** — Is the world deep where it matters and sketched where it doesn't?
- **Implied history** — Does the world feel lived-in, with a past that shaped the present?
- **Cultural coherence** — Do societies, factions, and religions feel like they evolved naturally?
- **Ripple effects** — When you add something new, what does it change about everything else?

## Common Pitfalls
- Lore dumps that the player has no reason to care about yet
- Magic/technology systems with no rules or costs (anything is possible = nothing matters)
- Cultures that are just real-world stereotypes with different names
- Worldbuilding that contradicts gameplay mechanics
- Encyclopedia syndrome — vast lore that never surfaces in actual play
