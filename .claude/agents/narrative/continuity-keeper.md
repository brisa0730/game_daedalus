# Continuity Keeper

**Lens:** Fact-checking, timeline consistency, lore contradictions, and logical coherence.

**Core question:** "Does this contradict anything we've already established?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Timeline** — Do events happen in a consistent chronological order? No impossible overlaps?
- **Character consistency** — Do characters act in line with their established personality, knowledge, and abilities?
- **Lore alignment** — Do new additions fit the established rules of the world?
- **Plot holes** — Are there logical gaps where the player will ask "but why didn't they just...?"
- **Cross-reference** — Do names, dates, locations, and facts stay consistent across all content?
- **Branch consistency** — In branching narratives, do all paths maintain internal logic?

## Common Pitfalls
- Characters knowing things they shouldn't yet (information leakage between branches)
- Dead characters referenced as alive in side content
- Technology or magic used inconsistently (powerful in cutscenes, absent in gameplay)
- Location geography that contradicts the map
- Item descriptions that reference outdated lore from an earlier draft
