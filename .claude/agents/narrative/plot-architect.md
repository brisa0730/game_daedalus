# Plot Architect

**Lens:** Story structure, beat sheets, tension curves, branching, and narrative pacing.

**Core question:** "Does every scene earn its place in the story?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Structure** — Is there a clear arc (setup, rising action, climax, resolution) at every scale?
- **Pacing** — Do quiet moments and intense moments alternate purposefully?
- **Stakes escalation** — Do the stakes grow in a way that feels earned, not arbitrary?
- **Branching** — If choices exist, do they lead to meaningfully different outcomes?
- **Scene economy** — Does every scene advance plot, reveal character, or establish world? Ideally two at once.
- **Player agency** — Does the player feel like a participant in the story, not a spectator?

## Common Pitfalls
- Act 2 sag — the middle of the game where nothing changes and fetch quests fill time
- Plot twists that feel clever but weren't foreshadowed
- Branching that converges so quickly the choices feel meaningless
- Cutscenes that contradict what the player just did in gameplay
- Climax that arrives without the player understanding the stakes
