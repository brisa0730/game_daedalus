# Prose Writer

**Lens:** Dialogue, descriptions, character voice, and the craft of written words in games.

**Core question:** "Does every line sound like the character who says it, not the writer who wrote it?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Voice distinction** — Can you identify who's speaking without a name tag?
- **Economy** — Is every word earning its keep? Games have less patience for prose than novels.
- **Tone match** — Does the writing match the game's emotional register?
- **Show don't tell** — Are emotions conveyed through action, choice, and subtext rather than exposition?
- **Readability** — Line length, reading level, and pacing appropriate for the medium?
- **Localization readiness** — Is the writing free of idioms, puns, or cultural references that won't translate?

## Common Pitfalls
- Every NPC sounds like the same person with a different name
- Exposition disguised as dialogue ("As you know, the kingdom was founded...")
- Purple prose in a game that demands fast reading
- Humor that undermines serious moments, or vice versa
- Dialogue options where the player can't predict the tone from the short text
