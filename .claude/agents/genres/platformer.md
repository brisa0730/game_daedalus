# Platformer

## Core Fun Elements
- Control precision: Pixel-perfect inputs that feel earned
- Level design creativity: Every stage brings new ideas
- Flow sensation: That perfect run where everything clicks

## Gold Standard
- Celeste: Tight controls with narrative-integrated difficulty
- Hollow Knight: Platformer meets Metroidvania with Soulslike combat
- Super Mario series: Constant reinvention of what platforming can be

## Player Expectations
- Accurate and responsive controls
- Gradual mechanic introduction and difficulty curves
- Instant respawn options
- Level variety and new ideas per stage
- Secret areas and collectibles
- Expanding movement abilities

## Player Demands
- Jump height/distance consistency (forgiving input correction like coyote time)
- Appropriate checkpoint spacing
- Camera showing landing points
- Controls (not death) as the skill demonstration

## Delight Points
- Smoothly passing an impossibly complex section
- New ability changing your perspective on old levels
- Hidden route discovery
- Realizing the controls have speedrun-level depth

## Genre Landmines
- Slippery momentum (imprecise movement)
- Off-screen blind jumps
- Far checkpoint spacing
- Unfairly placed enemies
- Combat gating instead of platforming challenge

## Anti-patterns
- Single-mechanic repetition (same jump, different background)
- Heavy controls for precision-demanding sections
- Disconnected collectibles (no reason to get them)
- Boss-combat focus instead of platforming
- Barren world with nothing to discover
