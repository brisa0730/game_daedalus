# Survival

## Core Fun Elements
- Resource scarcity tension: Never quite enough, always managing
- Growing base satisfaction: Watching your shelter evolve into a fortress
- Environmental mastery: Conquering a hostile world through knowledge

## Gold Standard
- Subnautica: Exploration-driven survival with narrative pull
- Valheim: Co-op survival with satisfying building and biome progression
- Don't Starve: Harsh survival with deep crafting and seasonal mechanics

## Player Expectations
- Clear gather→craft→build loop
- Expanding crafting recipes over time
- Base construction freedom
- Environmental threats
- Exploration-driven new content
- Co-op support

## Player Demands
- Clear progression feeling (stone axe → iron axe is tangible)
- Functionally valuable bases (not just cosmetic)
- Balanced system complexity (not overwhelming)
- Appropriate death penalties
- Co-op support (or satisfying solo experience)

## Delight Points
- First shelter completion feeling
- Near-death survival stories
- Returning from dangerous expedition with rare resources
- Shared base growth with friends

## Genre Landmines
- Endless farming loops (gather same things forever)
- Nightly zombie wave repetition
- Offline base destruction
- Excessive resource gauge management (hunger/thirst/temp/stamina/...)
- Early difficulty spikes that feel unfair

## Anti-patterns
- "Starvation simulators" (only managing meters, no fun)
- Opaque crafting recipes (no way to discover without wiki)
- Tooltip-only tech trees (no experimentation)
- Post-mid-game threat disappearance
- Rigid building systems (can't build what you want)
