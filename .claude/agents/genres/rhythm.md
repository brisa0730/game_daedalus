# Rhythm

## Core Fun Elements
- Beat synchronization euphoria: The rush of perfectly matching the music
- Song selection: A library that makes you want to play
- Progression feeling: Visible skill improvement over time

## Gold Standard
- Rhythm Heaven: Pure rhythm distilled to its essence
- Hi-Fi Rush: Rhythm integrated into action gameplay
- Muse Dash: Accessible rhythm with style and variety

## Player Expectations
- Precise input-music synchronization
- Diverse and appealing song library
- Difficulty tiers (easy to expert)
- Fair and clear judgment system
- Score/rank systems
- Music-reactive visuals

## Player Demands
- Minimal audio latency
- Consistent judgment timing
- Clear note readability
- Fast retries without loading screens

## Delight Points
- Perfect clears (full combo/all perfect)
- Musical flow state (becoming one with the song)
- Hard song first clear
- Discovering new music genres through gameplay

## Genre Landmines
- Input lag / audio desync
- Judgment-display mismatch (visual doesn't match audio)
- Overly busy visuals obscuring notes
- Limited or off-genre song selection
- DLC-locked songs fragmenting the experience

## Anti-patterns
- "Eye-reading" games (read visual, ignore music)
- Overly lenient judgment (no skill expression)
- Missing popular/expected songs
- Grind-locked song access
- Input method doesn't match notation style
