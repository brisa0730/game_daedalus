# Open World

## Core Fun Elements
- Exploration freedom: See it, go there, discover something
- Discovery wonder: The joy of finding the unexpected
- World density: A living, detailed world worth exploring

## Gold Standard
- BotW/TotK: Physics-driven exploration with emergent solutions
- Elden Ring: Open world that respects player skill and discovery
- RDR2: Unmatched world density and living ecosystem

## Player Expectations
- Unlimited movement freedom (visible = reachable)
- Exploration rewards (secrets/viewpoints/unique items)
- Living world (NPC routines/dynamic weather/ecosystems)
- Rich side content
- Fast travel vs exploration choice
- Enjoyable traversal mechanics

## Player Demands
- Meaningful exploration payoff (no empty walking)
- Journey-to-destination fun (the trip matters, not just the goal)
- Clear navigation (not lost/frustrated)
- Consistent world density (no late-game barren zones)

## Delight Points
- Vista moments (reaching a peak and seeing the world spread below)
- Unexpected hidden dungeon/event discovery
- Distant quest connections clicking together
- Feeling the developer's care in small details

## Genre Landmines
- "Ubisoft icon hell" (map cluttered with meaningless markers)
- Empty space traversal padding
- Invisible walls blocking visible destinations
- XP/gold-only exploration rewards (no unique content)
- Copy-paste camps/bases

## Anti-patterns
- Large but hollow maps
- Minimap/marker dependency design
- Main quest divorced from the open world
- Level-locked exploration barriers
- Identical repeating structure content
