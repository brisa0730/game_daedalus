# Strategy

## Core Fun Elements
- Reading the board: Assessing complex situations
- Decision-making: Every choice has meaningful trade-offs
- Long-term planning: Decisions made now pay off (or backfire) later

## Gold Standard
- Civilization: "One more turn" through satisfying long-term planning
- XCOM: Perfect blend of strategic base management and tactical combat
- Into the Breach: Transparent systems enabling perfect information decision-making

## Player Expectations
- Information-based decision-making
- Short-term tactics and long-term strategy balance
- Multiple winning strategies
- Challenging and fair AI
- Meaningful turn/time value
- Strategic map variety
- High replayability

## Player Demands
- Decision weight (clear trade-offs)
- Information readability (UI that enables situation understanding)
- Non-cheating fair AI
- Failure-cause traceability (understand why you lost)

## Delight Points
- A foundation laid 10 turns ago paying off perfectly
- Reversing a tactical disadvantage
- Discovering genius system combinations
- Accurately reading opponent intent

## Genre Landmines
- AI that blatantly cheats (resource bonuses, map vision)
- Decision paralysis (too many options, no guidance)
- Single optimal meta strategy
- Late-game busywork (maintenance with no new decisions)
- RNG invalidating strategy

## Anti-patterns
- Decision-free waiting games (time passes, things happen)
- Early-lead snowball inevitability
- False complexity with shallow depth
- Identical optimal paths on replay
- Multiplayer ruined by quitting/surrendering
