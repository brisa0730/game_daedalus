# Social / Party

## Core Fun Elements
- Social communication: The game is a catalyst for interaction
- Betrayal/cooperation dynamics: Trust and deception create memorable moments
- Accessibility: Anyone can join, regardless of gaming experience

## Gold Standard
- Among Us: Social deduction perfected for the masses
- It Takes Two: Co-op mechanics that force meaningful cooperation
- Overcooked: Chaos-driven cooperation under pressure

## Player Expectations
- Rules learnable in under 30 seconds
- Non-gamers can participate
- Short sessions with natural replay desire
- Core fun comes from social interaction, not mechanics
- Process matters more than winning
- Flexible player counts (2-8+)

## Player Demands
- Immediate rule comprehension
- Single player failure doesn't break the game
- Turn-to-turn variability
- Fair for non-gamers (skill gap doesn't dominate)

## Delight Points
- Catching a betrayer in the act
- Near-impossible cooperation success
- Unexpected reversals
- Conversations continuing after the game ends

## Genre Landmines
- Single-player dominance (one person carries/ruins)
- Long elimination downtime (eliminated = watching)
- Complex ruleset (breaks the accessibility promise)
- Skilled-player unfair advantage
- Online communication dependency (doesn't work without voice chat)

## Anti-patterns
- Parallel mini-games (no player interaction)
- Shallow mini-game variety (played everything in 30 min)
- RNG-driven outcomes (skill/social reading irrelevant)
- Strict player count requirements
- Quick pattern exhaustion
