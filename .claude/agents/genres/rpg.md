# RPG

## Core Fun Elements
- Growth fulfillment: Character power curve where leveling and gear changes are palpable
- Build freedom: Depth of character building with multiple viable builds
- Narrative immersion: Agency where my choices affect the world

## Gold Standard
- BG3: Freedom where choices reshape the world — infinite approaches via D&D rules
- Persona 5: Perfected daily life-dungeon loop — time management and relationship building connect to combat power
- Divinity OS2: Systemic creativity meets narrative — elemental combinations open tactical freedom

## Player Expectations
- Tangible growth when leveling up or swapping gear
- Meaningful build choices (multiple viable builds)
- Immersive worldbuilding
- Meaningful NPC interactions
- Narrative context for quests
- Exploration rewards (hidden locations, rare items)
- Character customization (appearance or stats)

## Player Demands
- Satisfying growth curve (minimize level stagnation periods)
- Build diversity (multiple strategies, not one optimal solution)
- Player agency in story (feeling that choices matter)
- Comfortable inventory/equipment management

## Delight Points
- A choice made 40 hours ago paying off in the ending
- "This is insane" moments when build synergies explode
- NPCs remembering and reacting to your actions
- "Aha" moments discovering worldbuilding depth
- Character growth aligning with narrative growth

## Genre Landmines
- Auto-level scaling that eliminates growth feel
- "Fake choices" where options exist but outcomes are identical
- Information-dump worldbuilding (codex dependency)
- Forced XP/item grinding
- Tone mismatch between main story and side quests

## Anti-patterns
- "Checklist RPG" — map full of icons only
- No build respec + late-discovered trap builds
- Fragmented combat-exploration-narrative with no connection
- Meaningless gear swaps that only raise DPS numbers
- Incompetent companion AI
