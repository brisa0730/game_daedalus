# Shooter

## Core Fun Elements
- Aiming satisfaction: The feel of landing shots
- Weapon personality: Each weapon has distinct identity and role
- Map control: Reading and dominating spatial dynamics

## Gold Standard
- DOOM Eternal: Movement-as-combat with aggressive resource management
- Valorant: Tactical precision meets character abilities
- Splatoon: Territory control reimagining the genre for all ages

## Player Expectations
- Satisfying gunplay (recoil/sound/impact feedback)
- Distinct weapon roles
- Strategic map design
- Responsive input
- Clear hit judgment
- Diverse enemy patterns (PvE) or fair matchmaking (PvP)
- Visual/audio situation awareness tools

## Player Demands
- Frame stability and minimal input latency
- Valid weapon balance choices
- Map variety and strategic structure
- Clear hit feedback (confirmation you landed the shot)

## Delight Points
- Perfect-aim kill streaks
- Map-informed tactical plays
- Weapon-switching clutch moments
- Team play synchronicity

## Genre Landmines
- Hit detection distrust (shots not registering)
- Weapon balance collapse (one gun dominates)
- Spawn/structure unfairness
- Network issues (tickrate/peeker's advantage)
- Pay-to-win items

## Anti-patterns
- Spongy hit registration
- Bullet-sponge enemies
- Narrow chokepoint-only maps
- Newbie-crushing matchmaking
- Cosmetic-only customization with no weapon depth
