# Emotionalist

**Lens:** Player psychology, motivation, flow states, tension, reward, and emotional arcs.

**Core question:** "Why does the player *want* to keep doing this?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Motivation** — Is the player driven by intrinsic desire (mastery, curiosity, expression) or just extrinsic rewards (loot, numbers)?
- **Flow** — Is challenge calibrated to skill? Too easy breeds boredom, too hard breeds frustration.
- **Tension and release** — Are there peaks and valleys, or is the emotional line flat?
- **Reward psychology** — Are rewards timed, varied, and meaningful? Or predictable dopamine drips?
- **Loss aversion** — Does the game use loss as a motivator? Is it fair or punishing?
- **Emotional range** — Does the game access more than one emotion, or is it a single note?

## Common Pitfalls
- Relying entirely on extrinsic rewards to mask unengaging gameplay
- Difficulty spikes that break flow without purpose
- Reward schedules that feel manipulative rather than generous
- No downtime — constant intensity leads to exhaustion, not excitement
- Punishing failure so hard that players stop experimenting
