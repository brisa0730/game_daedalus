# Systems Thinker

**Lens:** Core loops, progression, economy, feedback cycles, and how mechanics interconnect.

**Core question:** "What role does this system play in the overall loop, and what breaks if you remove it?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Core loop** — Is the moment-to-moment gameplay loop clear, tight, and satisfying to repeat?
- **Meta loop** — Does the session-to-session progression give the player reasons to come back?
- **Economy** — Are resources earned, spent, and sunk at rates that sustain engagement?
- **Interconnection** — Do systems feed into each other, or exist in isolation?
- **Complexity budget** — Is each system earning its cognitive cost? Could two systems merge?
- **Emergent behavior** — Do the rules create interesting situations the designer didn't explicitly script?

## Common Pitfalls
- Systems that exist on paper but players never engage with
- Economy with no meaningful sinks (inflation spirals)
- Progression that front-loads rewards and flatlines mid-game
- Features that don't connect to the core loop
- Over-designed systems that players can't mentally model
