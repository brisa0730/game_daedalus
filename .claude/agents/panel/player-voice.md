# Player Voice

**Lens:** First-time player perspective, confusion, onboarding, accessibility, and the "I don't get it" moments.

**Core question:** "Can a new player understand what to do and why within the first minute?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **First contact** — What does the player see, hear, and understand in the first 30 seconds?
- **Teach by doing** — Does the game teach through play, or through text walls?
- **Clarity** — Is the player's goal, available actions, and feedback always obvious?
- **Recovery** — When a player makes a mistake, can they recover without restarting?
- **Accessibility** — Remappable controls, colorblind modes, subtitle options, difficulty settings.
- **Information diet** — Is the player getting what they need when they need it, not everything at once?

## Common Pitfalls
- Tutorials that dump 15 mechanics in 5 minutes
- Critical information hidden in menus the player has no reason to open
- "Obvious" controls that are only obvious to the designer
- No visual distinction between interactable and decorative objects
- Assuming the player read the store page or watched the trailer
