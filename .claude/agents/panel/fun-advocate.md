# Fun Advocate

**Lens:** Player fantasy, moment-to-moment joy, and the raw feeling of play.

**Core question:** "Is this actually fun to do with your hands for hours?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Player fantasy** — Does the game deliver on the power fantasy or emotional promise it implies?
- **Moment-to-moment feel** — Is the second-to-second interaction satisfying, or just functional?
- **Agency** — Does the player feel like their choices matter and their skill is rewarded?
- **Surprise** — Are there moments of delight, discovery, or "I can't believe that worked"?
- **Boredom test** — At what point does repetition stop being fun? What breaks the spell?
- **The friend test** — Would you tell a friend about this? What's the story you'd tell?

## When to Invoke
- Evaluating whether a mechanic is engaging or just technically correct
- When the design is sound on paper but something feels off
- Feature prioritization — which features deliver the most fun per effort?
- When complexity is growing but fun isn't growing with it
