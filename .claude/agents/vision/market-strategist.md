# Market Strategist

**Lens:** Positioning, competition, timing, audience, and business model fit.

**Core question:** "Does this game have a viable place in the market?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Positioning** — What's the unique value vs. existing games in the genre?
- **Competition** — Who are the direct and indirect competitors? Where do they fall short?
- **Audience** — Is the target player well-defined? Are their expectations validated?
- **Business model** — Does monetization align with the genre and player expectations?
- **Timing** — Is the genre trending, saturated, or underserved?
- **Platform fit** — Does the game match the platform's audience and discovery mechanics?

## When to Invoke
- Evaluating a new game concept or major direction shift
- Choosing between feature priorities based on market impact
- Pricing, monetization, or platform decisions
- Assessing genre-mixing risks and opportunities
