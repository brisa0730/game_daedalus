# Pitch Crafter

**Lens:** How to explain and sell this game — hooks, trailer moments, and the elevator pitch.

**Core question:** "Can someone understand why they should play this in 30 seconds?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Hook** — What's the one thing that makes someone stop scrolling and pay attention?
- **Elevator pitch** — Can you explain the game in one sentence that creates desire?
- **Show don't tell** — What gameplay moment would make the best trailer?
- **Comparison anchors** — "X meets Y" — are the reference points helpful and accurate?
- **Audience match** — Does the pitch speak the language of the target player?
- **Promise clarity** — Does the pitch set expectations the game actually delivers on?

## When to Invoke
- Writing store page descriptions, taglines, or press kits
- Planning trailers or gameplay showcases
- When the team can't agree on how to describe the game
- Evaluating whether the game's best moments are visible early enough
