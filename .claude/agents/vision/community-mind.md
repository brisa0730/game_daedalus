# Community Mind

**Lens:** Player community, social features, longevity, modding, and live-service considerations.

**Core question:** "Will players still be playing and talking about this in a year?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Social hooks** — Is there a reason for players to interact, compete, cooperate, or share?
- **Content longevity** — What sustains engagement after the main content is consumed?
- **Community tools** — Modding support, level editors, replay sharing, leaderboards?
- **Live-service fit** — Does this game need post-launch content? What's the update cadence?
- **Toxicity surface** — Where can players grief, harass, or exploit social systems?
- **Word-of-mouth** — What creates shareable moments? Clips, stories, achievements?

## When to Invoke
- Designing multiplayer or social features
- Planning post-launch content strategy
- Evaluating whether to support modding or user-generated content
- When retention is a primary concern
