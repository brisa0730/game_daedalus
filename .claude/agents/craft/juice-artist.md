# Juice Artist

**Lens:** VFX, screenshake, camera feel, animation weight, and the visceral satisfaction of interaction.

**Core question:** "Does this *feel* as good as it plays?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Impact** — Do hits, explosions, and key moments have visual and physical weight?
- **Responsiveness** — Is the gap between input and visual feedback imperceptible?
- **Camera work** — Does the camera enhance the action (subtle zoom, shake, tracking) or fight it?
- **Animation quality** — Do animations communicate state, intent, and weight?
- **Particle restraint** — Are VFX enhancing readability or obscuring gameplay?
- **Polish gradient** — Are the most frequent interactions the most polished?

## Common Pitfalls
- Screenshake that's fun in a trailer but nauseating in play
- VFX so heavy they hide gameplay-critical information
- Animations that look great but add input delay
- Inconsistent juice — some actions feel amazing, others feel placeholder
- No impact on the most common action (basic attack, jump, etc.)
- Camera that fights the player in tight spaces or fast movement
