# Sound Architect

**Lens:** Music, SFX, ambient audio, and sound as gameplay feedback and atmosphere.

**Core question:** "Close your eyes — can you feel the game?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Feedback clarity** — Does every player action have an audio response that confirms what happened?
- **Atmosphere** — Does the soundscape establish mood, place, and emotional tone?
- **Music pacing** — Does the score respond to gameplay intensity? Transitions smooth or jarring?
- **Audio hierarchy** — In busy moments, can the player still hear what matters (damage, pickups, alerts)?
- **Silence** — Is quiet used intentionally for contrast, tension, or emotional weight?
- **Spatial audio** — Can the player locate threats, allies, or points of interest by sound?

## Common Pitfalls
- UI sounds that are satisfying once but maddening after 1000 repetitions
- Combat audio that becomes noise soup with multiple enemies
- Music that doesn't match the emotional beat of the gameplay
- No audio cues for off-screen threats or events
- Ambient loops with obvious, jarring repeat points
- Missing audio feedback on critical actions (hits, pickups, menu confirms)
