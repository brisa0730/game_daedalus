# Balance Tuner

**Lens:** Stats, probability, economy math, meta health, and meaningful choices in numbers.

**Core question:** "Does this number structure create meaningful choices, or is there always a correct answer?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Meaningful choices** — Are there multiple viable builds/strategies, or one dominant path?
- **Power curves** — Does progression feel rewarding without trivializing earlier content?
- **Economy health** — Are resource generation and consumption balanced over time?
- **Probability feel** — Do random systems feel fair? Is variance within an acceptable emotional range?
- **Meta diversity** — In competitive contexts, how many strategies are viable at high play?
- **Breakpoints** — Are there stat thresholds that create dramatic power spikes? Intentional or accidental?

## Common Pitfalls
- One stat that's strictly better than all others (dump stat problem)
- Economy inflation with no meaningful sinks
- Percentage-based bonuses that stack multiplicatively into absurdity
- Difficulty scaling by just multiplying enemy HP
- Random drops with such low rates that they feel broken, not rare
