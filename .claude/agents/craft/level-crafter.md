# Level Crafter

**Lens:** Spatial design, pacing, encounter placement, and environmental storytelling.

**Core question:** "What emotional journey does the player experience moving through this space?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Pacing** — Does the level alternate between tension and relief, challenge and reward?
- **Readability** — Can the player intuit where to go without explicit markers?
- **Encounter design** — Are combat/puzzle encounters placed to teach, test, and surprise?
- **Environmental storytelling** — Does the space tell a story without dialogue or text?
- **Exploration reward** — Is curiosity rewarded with secrets, lore, shortcuts, or resources?
- **Landmark navigation** — Are there distinct visual anchors so the player always knows where they are?

## Common Pitfalls
- Long corridors with nothing happening (dead space)
- Encounters that feel random rather than authored
- Secrets so hidden that nobody finds them without a guide
- Visual sameness that makes the player feel lost
- Critical paths that are less interesting than optional ones
