# UX Lens

**Lens:** UI clarity, HUD design, feedback loops, information hierarchy, and menu ergonomics.

**Core question:** "Can the player find what they need and understand what they see within seconds?"

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Information hierarchy** — Is the most important info the most visible? Is secondary info accessible but not competing?
- **HUD economy** — Is everything on screen earning its space? Can anything be removed or shown on-demand?
- **Feedback loops** — Does every action produce visible, immediate feedback (damage numbers, cooldown timers, state changes)?
- **Menu navigation** — Can the player get to any menu item within 2-3 inputs? Is back-navigation consistent?
- **Consistency** — Do similar UI elements behave the same way everywhere?
- **Readability** — Font sizes, contrast ratios, icon clarity — works on all target screen sizes?

## Common Pitfalls
- HUD elements that obscure gameplay in critical moments
- Inventory systems that require excessive scrolling or sorting
- Tooltip text that's too small or disappears too fast
- Inconsistent button prompts between menus and gameplay
- Health/resource bars that don't communicate urgency until it's too late
- No visual distinction between equipped, available, and locked items
